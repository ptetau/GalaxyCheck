# Review of S3 (number generators), round 2

- minor: [requirement] src/GalaxyCheck/Gens/RealGens.cs:181 With a bound or an origin of a large magnitude, the shifted value rounds to the origin or falls back to the bound. `Gen.Double().GreaterThanEqual(1e308)` gives 5 different values in 1000, and 88% of them are the bound. `Gen.Double().ShrinkTowards(double.MaxValue)` gives 10 different values, and 80% of them are the bound. `Gen.Single().GreaterThanEqual(1e38f)` gives 15. Fix: when the origin is large compared with the width, draw exponents near the exponent of the width, so that the shift does not round away.
- minor: [requirement] src/GalaxyCheck/Gens/RealGens.cs:143 New with the fix: with a narrow range whose origin is a bound, about half of the values are the origin. `Gen.Double().Between(1, 1.0000001)` gives 1 for 49% of 2000 values, and `Gen.Single().Between(0.1f, 0.2f)` gives 0.1 for 47%. The whole kind can only give 0 when the width is below one, and the exponents below the center give a value that rounds to the origin. The values stay in the bounds. Fix: skip the whole kind when the width is below one, and draw no exponent so small that the shift rounds to the origin.
- minor: [requirement] src/GalaxyCheck/Gens/Internal/RealFormat.cs:148 R10 (decimal): a finite decimal is at most `ulong.MaxValue` times 10^9, about 1.8e28. The values from 1.8e28 to 7.9e28 appear only as `decimal.MinValue` and `decimal.MaxValue` (0 of 2000 values of `Gen.Decimal()`). Fix: draw a 96-bit mantissa, or allow the exponent 10 with a mantissa limit.
- minor: [requirement] src/GalaxyCheck/Gens/RealGens.cs:99 An infinite bound is accepted and clamped to the finite range. `Gen.Double().GreaterThanEqual(double.PositiveInfinity)` gives only `double.MaxValue`, with no error. Fix: give a generator error that names the option, as for a NaN bound, or document the clamp.
